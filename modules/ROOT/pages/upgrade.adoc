= Upgrading Anypoint Platform PCE v3.2.5 to v4.1.x
ifndef::env-site,env-github[]
include::_attributes.adoc[]
endif::[]


Before you upgrade Anypoint Platform Private Cloud Edition (Anypoint Platform PCE) to version 4.1, verify that your working installation meets any additional requirements of the new Anypoint Platform PCE version. If your current PCE version is earlier than 3.2.4, upgrade to 3.2.5 to minimize potential issues.



See the following topics:

* xref:index.adoc[]
* xref:install-checklist.adoc[]

[NOTE]
Contact MuleSoft professional services prior to upgrading. MuleSoft professional services must be involved throughout the upgrade process.
